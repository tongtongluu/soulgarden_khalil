Soul Garden · A tribute to Khalil Fong 方大同 · 1983–2025

Kauaʻi, Hawaiʻi→ Hong Kong→ Yunnan

Every song is a vessel holding the images in its lyrics. Its color comes from the images that appear most, and its curves come from the song's tempo and mood. Hover over a vessel to see what it holds, and turn the sound on to hear it.

Color
The leading image sets the base color. The second image sets the other end of the gradient.
Number of curves
The faster the tempo, the more the outline bends.
Depth of curves
The stronger the emotion, the narrower the waist.
Pebble shape
A slow ballad, under 78 BPM.
Background
The page fades through the places he lived as you scroll: the ocean of Kauaʻi where he was born, the harbor haze of Hong Kong where he grew up and made music, and the red earth of Yunnan where he went in his last years.
Blends
A group of songs averaged into one outline, with each image as a flowing patch of color sized by its share. Album blends show each album's character. Soul palette pours every song into one vessel.

Prototype: the albums, tracks and imagery shares are randomly generated placeholders for testing the visual rules. They will be replaced once the real lyrics are collected and tagged.
