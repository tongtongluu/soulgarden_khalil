Soul Garden · A tribute to Khalil Fong 方大同 · 1983–2025

Kauaʻi, Hawaiʻi→ Hong Kong→ Yunnan

Every song is a vessel holding the images in its lyrics. Its color comes from the images that appear most, and its curves come from the song's tempo and mood. Hover over a vessel to see what it holds, and turn the sound on to hear it.

Color
Every imagery word in the lyrics is counted into one of ten groups. Only things you can see count: abstract words such as 歲 (age) or 世界 (world), time words and verbs are left out. The group with the most hits sets the base color, the second sets the other end of the gradient.
Beat
The faster the tempo, the more curves in the outline. Hover a vase and it jumps on every beat at the song's real BPM, hardest on the first beat of each bar; with sound on it follows the kick drum in the music.
Height and width
Brighter, happier songs (valence) stand taller. Songs with more energy are fuller and wider.
Depth of curves
The stronger the emotion, the narrower the waist.
Flower
He sang about love all the time. Every song whose lyrics mention love 愛 gets flowers in its vase: one flower for every five mentions, and each petal is one mention, so you can count them.
Pebble shape
A slow ballad, under 78 BPM.
Background
The garden sits in the light of the places he lived: the ocean of Kauaʻi where he was born at the top, the harbor haze of Hong Kong where he grew up and made music across the middle, and the red earth of Yunnan where he went in his last years rising from below.
Song posters
One abstract poster per song, drawn from a scene card written from a close reading of its lyrics: its main image sits at the centre as one clear form, and the colours around it follow the mood and emotional arc. Each poster can be printed three ways: blend, pixel or geometric. Counts in the picture, such as lobes, rings, rainbow bands and jumping shapes, are how many times the lyrics sing them. The album poster comes first.
Dreamscape
Every imagery word, freed from its song and recombined into one picture of flat gradient shapes: moon, rainbow, lanterns, swallows, the sea. Each image is sized by how often he sings it; birds, butterflies, flowers, people, lanterns and stars repeat instead, so you can count them. Recompose shuffles the arrangement, and hovering an image plays the song that sings it most.
Blends
A group of songs averaged into one outline, with each image as a flowing patch of color sized by its share. Album blends show each album's character. Soul palette pours every song into one smiling bowl: the bowl holds his whole palette, the two fins carry the second and third images, and each leaf is one song. Hover a leaf to hear it.

Prototype: the albums, tracks and imagery shares are randomly generated placeholders for testing the visual rules. They will be replaced once the real lyrics are collected and tagged.
